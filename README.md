# Amazon India Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

A 4-page interactive Power BI dashboard built for a non-technical manager,
analysing 10,000 Amazon India orders (Jan 2024 to Aug 2026).

## Pages
1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance: KPIs and monthly trend
2. Category & Product Performance: category sales, profit, units, Top 10 products
3. Order Status & Revenue Loss: delivered, shipped, returned, cancelled orders and rates
4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance: payment methods, fulfillment types, states

## Key Insights
- Total sales of Rs 155.8M with a profit margin of about 21.3%
- Electronics & Mobiles drives about 79% of sales
- Returns and cancellations cost Rs 15.6M (about 10% of orders)
- UPI is the top payment method (about half of sales)
- Amazon FBA handles about 69% of sales; Maharashtra is the top state

## Tools
Power BI, Power Query, DAX
